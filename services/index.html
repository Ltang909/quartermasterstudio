No. 02

Venue Sourcing and Negotiation

We find the room and negotiate the contract. You sign direct, so the pricing leverage stays yours.

  • ✓ A shortlist of private rooms that fit the guest list, the format, and the budget. Enclosed rooms, long tables, no ballrooms.
  • ✓ Site visits handled. You see the final two, not the first twenty.
  • ✓ Contract negotiation: rates, minimums, cancellation terms, AV inclusions.
  • ✓ Every contract in your name. We never sit between you and the venue's invoice.

The rule: if we cannot beat what you would negotiate yourself, we say so.

No. 03

On-Site Coordination

Our team runs the room. Your team works it. Seating, courses, introductions, all on cue.

  • ✓ A run-of-show timed to the minute: arrivals, seating, courses, toasts, departures.
  • ✓ Seating that puts the right two people next to each other, and keeps the wrong two apart.
  • ✓ Vendor wrangling on the night. The AV works, the courses land, the room stays on time.
  • ✓ Backups for the backups: dietary misses, late arrivals, and no-shows handled before you notice.

The standard: the room runs on time, and your team never looks at a clock.

How we work

Three rules we never break

Rule one

You hold the contracts

Venue, food, AV, and gifting are contracted directly by you. Nothing is marked up. The saving is on the people, not the program.

Rule two

One number per dinner

Our fee is quoted per dinner booked, not per hour, per head, or per revision. The number you approve is the number you pay.

Rule three

The room is the product

One seating, introductions that matter. If it does not move a relationship forward, it was catering, not strategy.
