The quartermaster standard

Three rules we never break

Rule one

You hold the contracts

Venue, food, AV, and gifting are contracted directly by you. You keep the pricing leverage, nothing is marked up, and the saving is on the people, not the program.

Rule two

One number per dinner

Our fee is quoted per dinner booked, not per hour, per head, or per revision. The number you approve is the number you pay.

Rule three

The room is the product

Twenty guests, one seating, introductions that matter. If an event does not move a relationship forward, it was catering, not strategy.
